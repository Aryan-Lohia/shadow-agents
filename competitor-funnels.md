# Competitor funnel research — Shadow Agents v2

Researched 13 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta). Patterns only. No competitor copy, claims, prices, or testimonials are reused on the Shadow Agents site. Shadow Agents still does not publish pricing, so the funnel leads with a free sample and a booking call — never a fabricated rate card.

---

## 1. Vidchops (vidchops.com / white-label)

**Who:** Credit-based editing subscription with a white-label portal for agencies.

**Funnel**
- **Hero hook:** Outcome for the agency principal — “we edit for your clients, you stay hands-off and make money.” Not “we are a video editor.”
- **Offer:** Dedicated editor, 1–2 day turnaround, portal where *their* clients submit/review/approve under the agency brand, they hire and train editors as you scale.
- **Lead magnet / trial:** 14-day money-back guarantee; some mentions of a short trial after a call.
- **Proof:** Named creator testimonials with roles; “trusted by” logo row; portfolio link; founder origin story mid-page.
- **Pricing visibility:** Public plans ($495 / $995 and a full-time editor). Clarity beats mystique for productized editing.
- **CTA pattern:** Sign-up / start, repeated after proof and after the include-list.
- **Urgency:** Fast TAT (2 days, 1-day revisions) rather than fake countdown clocks.
- **Guarantee:** 14-day money back, no contract, cancel anytime.
- **Booking flow:** Pay → portal account → onboarding questions → editor match within hours → Drive + request card.

**Borrow:** Lead with the agency outcome, not the service label. Put the guarantee in the hero, not only in the footer. Repeat a single primary CTA. Do not invent a price.

## 2. Video Husky (videohusky.com)

**Who:** Done-for-you editing platform for creators and production houses; white-label / dedicated-team quote for agencies.

**Funnel**
- **Hero hook:** Problem-relief — “edited right, 100% stress free” — plus a live counter of creators and videos edited, immediately under the headline.
- **Offer:** Dedicated account manager + editor, project dashboard, unlimited requests on one plan, credit bundles on another.
- **Lead magnet:** 14-day money-back; custom team quote for white-label.
- **Proof:** Proof is the *first* supporting module — volume stats, then named testimonials with companies, then a work reel. Reviews live on a dedicated page.
- **Pricing:** Plans exist (from around $849/mo in third-party writeups); homepage sells the model first, quote second.
- **CTA:** “Get your videos edited” / “Meet your new personal video editing team.”
- **Urgency:** “97.5% of requests in 2 business days” — a specific delivery claim.
- **Guarantee:** 14-day money back; month-to-month, no long lock-in.
- **Booking flow:** Join → upload footage + instructions → first draft 1–3 days → revise → next project. Always a 3-step diagram.

**Borrow:** Place proof (stats + quotes) *above* the long feature list. Keep a ruthless 3- or 4-step process. Humanise with the founder, but after the offer is clear.

## 3. EditClub (editclub.co)

**Who:** Subscription “video editing agency” for DTC brands and creators (volume social/ads).

**Funnel**
- **Hero hook:** Category + mechanism in one line: “works like your in-house team, priced like a subscription.” Four proof chips under the fold (guarantee, price, brand count, rating).
- **Offer:** Dedicated editor + PM, unlimited requests, 48h delivery, flat monthly fee.
- **Lead magnet / risk reversal:** “First edit in 48h or the month is on us.”
- **Proof:** Rating + review count, then quotes with names and titles. (We will not invent any of this.)
- **Pricing:** $1,600/mo, cancel anytime — shown in the hero.
- **CTA:** Direct and repeated; qualifying “this isn’t for everyone” section *before* the close, which actually increases conversion by filtering tire-kickers.
- **Urgency:** Delivery guarantee, not scarcity theatre.
- **Guarantee:** 48h or it’s free / month on us.
- **Booking flow:** Implied subscribe; comparison table of in-house vs freelancer vs EditClub sits mid-funnel as the decision tool.

**Borrow:** A visible for/not-for block. A comparison table (in-house vs freelancers vs us) as a mid-funnel decision aid. Risk reversal stated as a badge, not a footnote. We already have the raw material for all three.

## 4. ContentBuck (contentbuck.com — agencies page)

**Who:** White-label unlimited editing for marketing agencies, flat monthly rate.

**Funnel**
- **Hero hook:** “Any client video, edited fast, flat monthly rate.” Four stat tiles: edits across clients, 24h TAT, dedicated editors per client, flat rate.
- **Offer:** White-label under the agency brand; editors who learn each client’s guidelines so account managers stop managing freelancers.
- **Lead magnet:** Not a free edit on this page — the hook is operational relief.
- **Proof:** Benefit tiles more than quotes on the agency URL.
- **Pricing:** Full public tier table on a dedicated pricing page ($999 / $2,999 / $5,499).
- **CTA:** Start / talk, with the number already known.
- **Urgency:** 24-hour (12-hour on the top plan).
- **Booking flow:** Submit on a project board; revisions in 24 hours.

**Borrow:** Speak to the account-manager pain (“stop managing freelancers”). Stat tiles that describe the *system*, not just vanity numbers.

## 5. Editvideo.io

**Who:** Dedicated full-time white-label editors and editor pods for agencies.

**Funnel**
- **Hero hook:** Consistency and secrecy — 100% white label, no watermarks, the agency keeps the relationship and the margin.
- **Offer:** Dedicated editor or senior-led pod; “workflow memory” (the editor learns each client).
- **Lead magnet:** **Free 90-second test edit.** This is the strongest trial pattern in the set.
- **Proof:** Process and model more than homepage testimonials in the sources reviewed.
- **Pricing:** Public (full-time from $2,950/mo; short-form packs also listed).
- **CTA:** Test edit first, then a plan.
- **Guarantee / trial:** The free test *is* the risk reversal.
- **Booking flow:** Test → dedicated editor relationship.

**Borrow:** Make “Get a free sample edit” the primary lead magnet (Shadow Agents already offers this — it was buried in an FAQ). Put it in the hero, the sticky bar, and the close.

## 6. ContentFellow (contentfellow.com/agencies)

**Who:** UK white-label partner for agencies; credit plans plus optional shoots.

**Funnel**
- **Hero hook:** Straight partner framing with a “quick answer” block (price, TAT, what a credit is).
- **Offer:** Monthly credit plans, rollover, no long contract, white-label add-ons.
- **Lead magnet:** **5 free credits for new partners.**
- **Proof:** Plan table does the selling; simple and numeric.
- **Pricing:** Fully visible.
- **CTA:** Become a partner / start with free credits.
- **Urgency:** None fake; rollover is the retention hook.
- **Booking flow:** Credits → delivery in 2–3 days.

**Borrow:** A no-risk first step (their free credits = our free sample). Be explicit about who a “partner” is.

## 7. The Editor’s Connection (theeditorsconnection.com)

**Who:** Unlimited editing engine aimed at agencies and creators.

**Funnel**
- **Hero hook:** Emotional and operational — “OUTSOURCE THE PAIN.” Amplify client satisfaction, simplify scaling.
- **Offer:** Unlimited requests, next-day shorts, 2–3 day other work, 80+ contracted editors, dedicated project support.
- **Lead magnet:** Not prominent in the public copy reviewed.
- **Proof:** “Who we work with” personas (agencies, creators, educators, brands, real estate) rather than only quotes.
- **Pricing:** Not foregrounded on the homepage excerpt.
- **CTA:** Get started / scale with demand.
- **Urgency:** Volume language (“20 campaigns at once”).
- **Booking flow:** Upgrade as demand grows.

**Borrow:** A sharp, agency-native hook. A “who this is for” persona strip. We already have a clear not-for line — surface it.

## 8. PreVetted Podcast — “Become a guest” (prevetted.fm)  
*(B2B / founder podcast landing page)*

**Funnel**
- **Hero hook:** Selective but open — “By invitation, mostly. By application, sometimes.” Positions the show, then invites a short application.
- **Offer to the guest:** A serious long-form conversation; they do not sell merch in the hero.
- **Lead magnet:** Free to appear (stated in the FAQ).
- **Proof:** Episode count, host title, archive, and “what every guest brings.”
- **CTA:** Application form in the middle of the page, not a vague “get in touch.”
- **Process:** 4 numbered “what happens next” steps with timing.
- **FAQ:** Format, remote recording, prep, cost, what if you’re not selected.

**Also observed on similar shows (Winners Circle, Agency Uplift):** eligibility criteria, a short apply form or Calendly, and a clear “what you get” (distribution, clips, a page).

**Borrow:** Treat the podcast page as a *guest-application funnel*, not a brochure. Benefits first, episodes as proof, process with timing they already publish (15–20 min intro, 30–40 min record), then a single apply CTA (their Calendly). State that it is free — they already do.

## Patterns we are adopting (and what we will not invent)

| Pattern | How it shows up in v2 | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome hook in the hero | Agency scaling without hiring / freelance chaos | No invented results |
| Risk reversal in the first screen | Free sample edit + “if it’s off-brief, you don’t pay” | Both already on the live site |
| Lead magnet as primary CTA | “Get a free sample edit” | Routes to contact / Calendly / WhatsApp |
| Proof placed high | Stats strip + a testimonial peek under the hero | Only real numbers and quotes |
| Comparison table | Freelancers vs in-house vs Shadow Agents | Left columns use only pains and offers already stated on their site |
| For / not-for | Qualifier mid-funnel | Existing FAQ copy |
| 3–4 step process | Meet → brand manager → production → feedback | From their workflow graphic |
| Sticky mobile CTA | Sample + Book a call | Real links |
| Guest-application podcast funnel | Benefits, episodes, host, process, apply | Existing Calendly and YouTube |
| Public pricing, countdown timers, named extra clients, “48h or it’s free” month-back, 14-day refund | **Not used** | They do not publish these |
