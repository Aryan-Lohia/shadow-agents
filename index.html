The system

Built to solve the root problems.

Not a bench of freelancers. A dedicated operating system: one point of contact, exclusive bandwidth, and quality checked before it ever reaches you.

Daily brand manager touchpoints

You get a dedicated Brand Manager who meets with you twice daily — once for new briefs and once for feedback. Your Brand Manager becomes your remote project manager.

Unlimited bandwidth system

A Dedicated Team handles your account exclusively. The Variable Team and the Emergency Team absorb workload spikes, so nothing slips.

Layered quality check

Every video asset is reviewed by the Team Lead, HOD, and your Brand Manager before it reaches you. No file is delivered without three human eyes confirming it.

We absorb the training cost

We cover the cost of onboarding and training new editors dedicated to your brand.

Under 12-hour turnaround

With a 12-hour turnaround guarantee, most standard edits come back the same day.

No payment for rejected work

If you don’t like it, you don’t pay for it. If the work is off-brief or unsatisfactory, you don’t pay.
